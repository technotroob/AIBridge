100% Privacy First

Your Conversations Stay Yours

We built AI Bridge with absolute privacy integrity. Your conversations are processed locally on your device solely to generate your continuation prompt.

  • No External Server Storage: Your chat logs are never sent to or stored on third-party servers.
  • Client-Side Processing: All extraction and formatting happens inside your browser's local extension sandbox.
  • Zero Accounts Required: No registration, email collection, or personal tracking.
  • You Control the Clipboard: Nothing is transferred without your explicit click.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AI Bridge and how it handles your sessions.

AI Bridge is a Chrome extension that enables seamless context continuation when switching between AI chat platforms. It scans your active conversation thread, extracts core goals, code, decisions, and constraints, and generates a structured prompt to paste into another AI model.
When you click the AI Bridge icon on an active ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, or DeepSeek tab, the extension reads the visible conversation DOM elements locally, parses user and assistant turns, extracts code blocks and key directives, and formats them into a clean markdown continuation prompt.
AI Bridge supports extraction and continuation for ChatGPT (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic), Gemini (Google), Grok (xAI), Perplexity AI, DeepSeek, and Microsoft Copilot.
Yes! AI Bridge core prompt generation and context transfer features are 100% free to install and use from the Chrome Web Store.
