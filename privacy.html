1. Introduction

Welcome to AI Bridge. We are committed to protecting your privacy and security. This Privacy Policy outlines how AI Bridge ("we", "our", or "us") processes information when you install and use the AI Bridge Microsoft Edge Extension.

We believe that your personal conversations and workspace data belong exclusively to you. Consequently, we have engineered AI Bridge from the ground up to follow strict client-side data minimization standards.

2. How AI Bridge Works

AI Bridge functions as a utility to help you bridge your workspace progress between distinct AI platforms (including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, DeepSeek, and Microsoft Copilot).

When you click the extension icon, AI Bridge queries the DOM structure of your active browser tab to read the conversation text. It extracts code sections, key user decisions, and target progress states to compile a structured, high-density continuation prompt, allowing you to paste it into another AI assistant.

3. Information We Collect

AI Bridge is designed to run without collecting personal identifiers. Here is a breakdown of what information is handled:

  • Conversation Data: The extension transiently reads the text of your active chat thread on supported AI websites. This data is processed temporarily in the browser sandbox and is never saved or cached permanently.
  • Extension Settings: The extension stores configurations (such as user preferences, theme options, or active platform list custom presets). This stays locally on your device.
  • API Keys (Optional): If you choose to enable cloud features (such as generating smart summaries via Google Gemini API), your private API key is stored locally and securely in Microsoft Edge's extension storage.

Minimalist Data Guarantee

AI Bridge does not collect your name, email, IP address, payment details, or browsing history outside of the active AI chat tabs you choose to interact with.

4. Data Processing Model

To maximize privacy, AI Bridge adheres to a **Local-First Execution Model**:

  • No Cloud Server Storage: Your conversation history is never transmitted to, processed by, or stored on our servers. We do not own or maintain databases storing user conversation history.
  • In-Browser Parsing: All extraction, text parsing, and continuation prompt formatting are executed entirely inside your browser's local sandbox using client-side JavaScript.
  • User Agency: The compiled continuation prompt is copied to your system clipboard or displayed in your browser. You remain fully responsible for the data you choose to paste and transfer to another AI platform.

5. Browser Permissions

When installing AI Bridge, your browser will request specific permissions. Here is what we use and why:

  • activeTab: Allows the extension to temporarily scan the DOM of your current active tab when you explicitly click the extension popup icon.
  • storage: Used to store your local preference settings, prompt presets, and optional API configurations.
  • Content Scripts access: Allows the extension to interact with supported AI domains (such as chatgpt.com or claude.ai) to read text bubbles for prompt creation.

6. Storage & Settings

AI Bridge utilizes browser local storage (specifically Microsoft Edge's chrome.storage.local or chrome.storage.sync API) to persist your workspace settings.

This data is sandboxed under your browser profile. If you uninstall the extension, all locally stored extension preferences and API keys are permanently deleted from your device.

7. Google Gemini API

AI Bridge offers an optional cloud summarization feature. If you explicitly enable this, the extension uses your self-provided Google Gemini API key to query Gemini models to generate high-density, smart continuation summaries.

If enabled:

  • Your conversation data is transmitted directly from your browser to Google API endpoints using secure HTTPS connections.
  • The data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's API Developer Terms.
  • We have no access to the keys or data payloads exchanged during this process. You can disable this feature at any time.

8. Third-Party Services

When you use AI Bridge to transfer context, you paste the generated prompts into third-party AI chat interfaces (such as Anthropic Claude, OpenAI ChatGPT, xAI Grok, or Perplexity).

Please note that these third-party platforms have their own privacy policies and terms of service. AI Bridge does not control and is not responsible for how those companies collect, train, or process prompts once you paste them into their interfaces.

9. No Selling of Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or conversation content. We have no interest in building profiles or selling tracking details. The project is sustained purely as a local utility extension.

10. Information Security

Because we do not transmit or store your conversations on remote servers, the risk of data breaches on our side is eliminated. To maintain security locally, we recommend:

  • Keeping your browser and Microsoft Edge extensions updated to the latest versions.
  • Safeguarding your device against unauthorized physical or digital access.
  • Avoiding pasting highly sensitive personal identifiers (such as passwords, credit card details, or security keys) into AI chats.

11. Your Rights & Updates

User Rights

Since we do not store any of your data, you do not need to request deletion or exports from us. You can clear all settings simply by removing the extension or clearing your browser's local cache.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make updates, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
